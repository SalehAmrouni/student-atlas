About

Opportunity shouldn't depend on who you know.

Many of the best programs for high schoolers are free or even pay students, yet countless students never hear about them. Student Atlas brings them together in one place and pairs them with Zeus, a built-in pathway guide that helps every student find a path, whether or not they have access to a college advisor.

How Student Atlas works

  • A curated directory. Hundreds of opportunities across seven interest areas, each linked to its official website and tagged with grades, cost, format, and typical deadline.
  • Explainable matching. Your Pathway profile is scored against every opportunity, and each card tells you why it fits, or what might make you ineligible.
  • Zeus, your pathway guide. Zeus is a predictive assistant built into Student Atlas. It reads your question, picks out what you're looking for (interest area, grade, cost, online or in person, timing), searches the directory, and ranks results with your profile. It remembers the conversation, so follow-ups like "only free ones" or "tell me about the second one" just work.
  • Community suggestions. Anyone can suggest a resource. Suggestions open as a GitHub issue and are reviewed by a person before anything is added.

How Zeus works

  • No AI service, no API key. Zeus runs entirely in your browser using a ranking engine (BM25 keyword search with typo tolerance) combined with your match scores and a library of counseling advice.
  • It only recommends real programs from the directory, and loosens filters out loud when nothing matches exactly.
  • It predicts what you're typing and suggests next questions after every answer.
  • Program details change every year, so always confirm dates, costs, and eligibility on the official site.
  • If you ever feel unsafe or overwhelmed, talk to a trusted adult. In the U.S. you can call or text 988 any time.

Privacy & security

  • No accounts, no passwords. Your profile, saved list, and settings are stored only in your browser. You can download or delete them anytime from My Pathway.
  • Chats never leave your device. Zeus runs in your browser, so your questions are not sent to our server or any AI company. Chat history clears when you close the tab.
  • Hardened by default. The site is fully static with a strict Content Security Policy and secure headers. There's no server-side code or database to break into.
  • No ads or trackers. We don't use third-party analytics or sell data.

Accessibility

Student Atlas aims to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA, the standard referenced by the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) Title II rule and Section 508 of the Rehabilitation Act. Here is what that looks like in practice:

  • Keyboard and screen readers. Every feature works without a mouse, focus is always visible, menus and dialogs close with Escape, and pages use landmarks, headings, and labels that screen readers announce.
  • Display settings. Use the button in the header to adjust text size, switch themes, turn on high contrast, use a readable font with extra spacing, swap to a color-blind friendly palette, reduce motion, or have the page read aloud. Your browser's own zoom (up to 400%), text spacing, and Windows High Contrast also work.
  • Phones and tablets. Pages reflow to a single column down to 320 pixels wide with no sideways scrolling, and buttons are large enough to tap.
  • Color and motion. Text meets AA contrast in light and dark themes, color is never the only way information is shown, and nothing moves or advances on its own.

Feedback. If something is hard to use with your device or assistive technology, please report an accessibility issue (opens in a new tab) and tell us the page, what you were trying to do, and the browser or assistive technology you use. We treat accessibility problems as bugs.
